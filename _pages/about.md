Hi, this is ***Yunnuo (Noah) Zhang***.

I am a Ph.D. student in [Machine Learning](https://ml.gatech.edu) at [Georgia Tech](https://www.gatech.edu) and Emory, and a trainee in the [Computational Neural-Engineering Training Program (CNTP)](https://cntp.bme.gatech.edu). I work with Dr. Chethan Pandarinath at the [Systems Neural Engineering Lab (SNEL)](https://www.snel.ai), and I am a research engineer with the [BrainGate](https://www.braingate.org) clinical trial team at Emory. My research focuses on building **high-performance, multifunctional intracortical brain-computer interfaces**, including speech neuroprostheses that generalize across users.

I received my B.S. (2024) and M.S. (2025) in Computer Science from Georgia Tech. As an undergraduate, I was advised by [Dr. Thad Starner](https://www.cc.gatech.edu/home/thad/index.htm) and [Dr. Melody Jackson](https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~melody/), and wrote my thesis on a [high-frequency SSVEP-BCI system](https://repository.gatech.edu/entities/publication/827501ba-d44f-4097-b261-2722073058ad) with wearable dry EEG electrodes. I also worked on soft, skin-conformal bioelectronics and machine learning for neural and physiological signals with [Dr. Jinxing Li](https://www.labli.net) at Michigan State University, whom I first worked with in the Bao Group at Stanford University. I have also collaborated with [Dr. Tingyu Cheng](https://tingyucheng.com/about) on applied AI, sustainable materials, and human-computer interaction.

I am interested in **Brain-Computer Interfaces**, **Computational Neuroscience**, **Neural Engineering**, and **Machine Learning for neural data**. Above all, I want to turn neuroscience and clinical research into technology that restores communication and independence for people living with paralysis.

See my [publications](/publications/) or [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=kfyTzPMAAAAJ) for my recent work.
